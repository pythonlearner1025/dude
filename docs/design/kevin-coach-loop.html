Kevin's Coach Loop

m8py never asks Opus for anything. Opus watches. About once a minute while you play, it reads everything since its last look: what you said, what Kevin said and did, his state, the frames you saw. Then it changes Kevin's skills, or leaves them alone.

The loop

There is no arrow from the actor to Opus. m8py writes everything to the session trace as it happens, and the coach reads the trace on its own heartbeat.

  • When it looks: at most once a minute, and only if, in a game, you spoke, a skill ended, the game reported something, or the actor made a real decision.
  • No queue: one look at a time. A look that takes two minutes makes the next packet two minutes long, so everything is batched by itself. A packet covers at most the last ten minutes.
  • When you stop: after 60 s with nothing in the trace, one last look at whatever is left.
  • Memory: notes.md in the library (read first, rewritten last), what it wrote last time, and its own commits since then. LIBRARY lines show when the game loaded each version, so it can tell whether a fix was live and failed.
  • What it fixes, in order: things you asked for that Kevin didn't do, then Kevin hurting your game (in your line of fire, dying, wandering off), then failing skills. One to three per look, or nothing.
  • Kevin hears about it: the commit subject is written for you. When the game loads the commit, Kevin can say it in his own words, and if he is running the skill that changed, it restarts on the new code.

The packet

Each look gets a directory, run/observations/<session>/<n>/, holding packet.json (the data), packet.md (the same data as one timeline, which is what Opus reads), the frames it lists, and afterwards Claude's log and result.md.

  • Quality: on "stop crouching", both Opus runs fixed the cause: every movement skill now stands Kevin up first. Both Sonnet runs added a separate stand_up skill instead, so Kevin still crouches while following unless the actor thinks to pick it. On "peek out" and on lead, all four found the problem and fixed it reasonably.
  • Where the time goes: each look is 12 to 40 tool calls, and every call is a full round trip. Fewer, bolder turns (lower effort) save more time than faster tokens. The packet helps here too: Opus no longer greps a 700 KB trace to find out what happened.
  • Setting: the coach now runs Opus 5.5 at medium effort. COACH_MODEL and COACH_EFFORT in start-coach.sh change it. One run per cell, so treat differences under about 20 s as noise.
